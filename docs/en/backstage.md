## under the hood

- Engine: **Pokémon Essentials v21.1** on **RPG Maker XP**.
- Game text fully in Italian (menus, battles, Pokédex, items, moves).
- Field moves kept their dialect names: **Tajo**, **Svolo**, **Sburton**, **Onda Granda**.
- Eight gym leaders, teams in `PBS/trainers.txt`, levels 12 to 50.
