**Pokémon Muia** is a fan game set entirely in Muia, with its places, its characters and its dialect.

## Story

1. **Molo dell'Illusion.** You have just arrived in Muia on the green dolphin. A strange guy gives you a few pointers and sends you to Polidori.
2. **Registry office.** Polidori explains a few things and lets you choose your first Pokémon.
3. **Around Muia.** You can talk to Dejan, who explains the rules of the game, or join Cinghio's gang or HarryDeLuca's. Your choice decides which benefits you get.

The story is still a work in progress: later scenes are yet to be written.

## Try it

There is no downloadable build yet: for now the game is worked on directly from the [repository](https://github.com/pokemonmuia/pokemon-muia). Contributors can open `Game.exe` to play, or `Game.rxproj` with RPG Maker XP to edit maps and events.
